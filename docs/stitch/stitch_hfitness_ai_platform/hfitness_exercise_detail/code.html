Barbell Incline Bench Press

Primary Muscle Focus: Upper Chest (Clavicular Pectoralis)
Secondary Kinetic Stabilizers:
Anterior Deltoids Triceps Brachii Serratus Anterior Core Stabilizers

Biomechanical Protocol

4 Phase Sequence
01

Set Up & Bench Positioning

Set the adjustable bench to a 30° to 45° angle. Sit down with hips and upper back firmly planted against the padding, retract and depress your scapulae, and plant your feet flat on the floor to establish a stable arch.

02

Grip & Unrack

Grasp the barbell with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder-width apart. Brace your core, squeeze the bar to activate forearm tension, and unrack the weight directly above your upper chest with elbows fully extended.

03

Controlled Eccentric Descent

Inhale deeply and lower the barbell smoothly over 3 seconds towards the clavicular line (upper chest), tucking elbows at approximately a 45-degree angle relative to your torso.

04

Concentric Press & Lockout

Drive through your palms and contract your upper pecs explosively to press the bar back up along a slight backward arc to the starting lockout position while maintaining shoulder retraction. Exhale at the peak.

Kinematic Safety & Injury Prevention

Mandatory
  • report_problem Avoid excessive bench angles above 45°, which shifts stress primarily onto the anterior deltoids and increases subacromial impingement risk.
  • report_problem Never bounce the barbell off your sternum or hyperextend wrists; keep wrists stacked directly over forearms throughout the entire range of motion.
  • report_problem Always engage safety catch bars at chest height when training without an active spotter.
