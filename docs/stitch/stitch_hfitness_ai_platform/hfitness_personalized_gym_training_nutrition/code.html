FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about HFitness

How does the AI personalize my workout plan?

check_circle

The platform analyzes your available gym equipment, training frequency, experience level, and biomechanical targets to build an individualized split with calibrated volume and tempo.

Can I use HFitness with standard gym equipment?

check_circle

Yes. HFitness lets you specify whatever equipment you have available—including barbells, dumbbells, cables, machines, or bodyweight.

How are nutrition targets calculated?

check_circle

Calorie and macro targets (protein, carbs, and fats) are mathematically derived from your body mass, activity volume, and specific physical objectives.

Do I need any specialized wearable hardware?

check_circle

No dedicated hardware or external sensors are required. All biomechanical protocols and tracking run directly through the web application.
