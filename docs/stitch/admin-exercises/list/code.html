Numbered Instructions

1

Position yourself on an incline bench set to 30–45 degrees with feet firmly planted flat on the floor.

2

Grip the barbell slightly wider than shoulder-width, retract your scapulae, and unrack the bar directly over your upper chest.

3

Inhale and lower the bar under control until it lightly touches your upper sternum, keeping elbows tucked at roughly 45–60 degrees.

4

Press the bar back up explosively along a slight backward curve until arms are fully extended without locking out elbows abruptly.
